# BS 812-105.1:1989 — Flakiness index

### Reading notes

This is a particle-shape test, not a PSD grading envelope. Clauses 3, 7 and 8: test the 6.30–63.0 mm fraction; discard size fractions at or below 5% of eligible mass M1; calculate flakiness index as 100 × M3 / M2 after exclusion. PSD masses alone cannot determine flaky mass M3.
