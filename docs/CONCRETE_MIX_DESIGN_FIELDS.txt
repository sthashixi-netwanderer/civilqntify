
================================================================================
SECTION 1: DESIGN STANDARD
================================================================================

STANDARD
--------
Description: Selects the calculation method for mix design.

Options:
  - ACI 211.1 (American Concrete Institute)
    Standard: ACI 211.1-91 "Standard Practice for Selecting Proportions
    for Normal, Heavyweight, and Mass Concrete"
    
  - IS 10262 (Bureau of Indian Standards)
    Standard: IS 10262:2019 "Code of Practice for Concrete Mix Design
    - An Additional Standard IS 456"

Note: Selecting a standard changes available options and calculation
      methodology. Some fields are standard-specific.

================================================================================
SECTION 2: MIX PARAMETERS
================================================================================

TARGET STRENGTH (MPa)
---------------------
Description: The characteristic compressive strength of concrete at 28 days.

ACI 211.1:
  - Called "Specified Compressive Strength" (f'c)
  - Range: Typically 17-70 MPa (2500-10000 psi)
  - Default: 25.0 MPa
  - Used to calculate required average strength (f'cr) with overdesign

IS 10262:
  - Called "Characteristic Compressive Strength" (fck)
  - Range: Typically 10-80 MPa
  - Default: 25.0 MPa
  - Target mean strength: f'ck + 1.65s (where s = standard deviation)

SLUMP (mm)
----------
Description: Measure of workability/consistency of fresh concrete.

  - Lower values = stiffer, less workable mix
  - Higher values = more fluid, easier to place

Typical ranges:
  - 25-50 mm:  Foundation concrete, road pavement
  - 50-100 mm: General structural concrete
  - 75-150 mm: Beams, columns, thin sections
  - 100-150 mm: Pumped concrete, heavily reinforced sections
  - 150-200 mm: Tremie concrete, underwater placement

Default: 75 mm

NMSA (Nominal Maximum Size of Aggregate)
----------------------------------------
Description: The largest aggregate size that will pass through most of
the aggregate particles.

Options:
  - 10 mm: Thin sections, heavily reinforced areas, pumps
  - 20 mm: General structural concrete (most common)
  - 40 mm: Mass concrete, foundations, gravity dams

Rule: NMSA should not exceed:
  - 1/5 the narrowest dimension between sides of forms
  - 1/3 the depth of slabs
  - 3/4 the clear spacing between reinforcing bars

Default: 20 mm

VOLUME (m³)
-----------
Description: Total volume of concrete to be designed.

  - Enter the total quantity of concrete needed for the project
  - All material quantities will be scaled to this volume
  - Enter 1.0 for per-cubic-meter design

Default: 1.0 m³

================================================================================
SECTION 3: CEMENT
================================================================================

TYPE
----
Description: Type of Portland cement or blended cement to be used.

ACI 211.1 Options:
  - TYPE_I: General purpose (OPC)
  - TYPE_II: Moderate sulfate resistance
  - TYPE_III: High early strength
  - TYPE_IV: Low heat of hydration
  - TYPE_V: High sulfate resistance

IS 10262 Options:
  - OPC_33: Ordinary Portland Cement 33 grade (IS 269)
  - OPC_43: Ordinary Portland Cement 43 grade (IS 8112) [Default]
  - OPC_53: Ordinary Portland Cement 53 grade (IS 12269)
  - PPC: Portland Pozzolana Cement (IS 1489)
  - PSC: Portland Slag Cement (IS 455)

Note: Cement type affects strength development and durability properties.

SPECIFIC GRAVITY
----------------
Description: Ratio of cement density to water density (25°C).

  - Typical range: 2.80 - 3.50
  - OPC: Usually 3.14 - 3.15
  - PPC: Usually 2.80 - 2.90
  - PSC: Usually 2.85 - 2.95

Used in: Absolute volume calculations

Default: 3.15

================================================================================
SECTION 4: FINE AGGREGATE (SAND)
================================================================================

SPECIFIC GRAVITY
----------------
Description: Ratio of fine aggregate density to water density.

  - Typical range: 2.20 - 3.00
  - Natural sand: 2.50 - 2.70
  - Manufactured sand: 2.60 - 2.90

Used in: Absolute volume calculations

Default: 2.65

FINENESS MODULUS (ACI 211.1 only)
---------------------------------
Description: An empirical figure obtained by adding the total percentages
of the sample of an aggregate retained on each of a specified series of
sieves, and dividing the sum by 100.

  - Lower FM = finer sand
  - Higher FM = coarser sand

Typical ranges:
  - Fine sand: 2.2 - 2.6
  - Medium sand: 2.6 - 2.9
  - Coarse sand: 2.9 - 3.2

Used in: ACI Table 6.3.6 - Choosing mixing water and air content

Default: 2.70

GRADING ZONE (IS 10262 only)
----------------------------
Description: Classification of fine aggregate based on particle size
distribution as per IS 383.

  - Zone I: Coarse sand
  - Zone II: Medium sand (most common) [Default]
  - Zone III: Fine sand
  - Zone IV: Very fine sand

Used in: IS Table 2 - Determination of coarse aggregate proportion

Note: Grading zone affects the proportion of fine to coarse aggregate.

ABSORPTION (%)
--------------
Description: The amount of water absorbed by aggregate to reach Saturated
Surface Dry (SSD) condition.

  - Clean sand: 0.5 - 2.0%
  - Typical: 1.0%

Used in: Moisture correction calculations
  - If aggregate absorption > free moisture: aggregate absorbs water
  - If aggregate absorption < free moisture: aggregate contributes water

Default: 1.0%

FREE MOISTURE (%)
-----------------
Description: Surface moisture content on fine aggregate (excess over SSD).

  - Positive: Wet aggregate (contributes water to mix)
  - Zero: SSD condition
  - Negative: Dry aggregate (absorbs water from mix)

Typical range: -2% to +5%

Default: 0.0% (SSD condition)

================================================================================
SECTION 5: COARSE AGGREGATE
================================================================================

SPECIFIC GRAVITY
----------------
Description: Ratio of coarse aggregate density to water density.

  - Typical range: 2.20 - 3.20
  - Gravel: 2.60 - 2.80
  - Crushed stone: 2.70 - 2.90

Used in: Absolute volume calculations

Default: 2.70

ABSORPTION (%)
--------------
Description: Amount of water absorbed by coarse aggregate to reach SSD.

  - Gravel: 0.2 - 1.0%
  - Crushed stone: 0.5 - 2.0%

Default: 0.5%

FREE MOISTURE (%)
-----------------
Description: Surface moisture on coarse aggregate (excess over SSD).

  - Same interpretation as fine aggregate
  - Typically lower than fine aggregate moisture

Default: 0.0% (SSD condition)

BULK DENSITY (kg/m³)
--------------------
Description: Dry rodded unit weight (DRUW) of coarse aggregate - mass of
aggregate that fills a container of known volume, rodded in standard manner.

  - Typical range: 1400 - 1800 kg/m³
  - Gravel: 1500 - 1700 kg/m³
  - Crushed stone: 1400 - 1600 kg/m³

Used in: ACI Method - Determining coarse aggregate volume per m³
  (ACI Table 6.3.6)

Default: 1600 kg/m³

AGGREGATE SHAPE (IS 10262 only)
-------------------------------
Description: Shape classification of coarse aggregate.

Options:
  - Gravel: Rounded or partially rounded natural aggregate
  - Crushed: Angular, mechanically crushed aggregate [Default]

Used in: IS Table 6 - Selection of fine aggregate proportion
  - Crushed aggregate requires more fines for same workability

Note: Affects water demand and workability.

================================================================================
SECTION 6: ACI OPTIONS (ACI 211.1 only)
================================================================================

AIR-EN TRAINED CONCRETE
------------------------
Description: Check if concrete will be air-entrained (contains deliberate
air voids for freeze-thaw resistance).

  - Unchecked: Normal concrete with entrapped air only
  - Checked: Air-entrained concrete with deliberate air bubbles

When checked:
  - Uses ACI Table 6.3.3 for air-entrained concrete
  - Typically 3-8% air content depending on exposure
  - Improves workability and freeze-thaw durability

SULFATE EXPOSURE
----------------
Description: Classifies sulfate exposure severity per ACI 318.

Options:
  - S0 — None: No sulfate exposure [Default]
  - S1 — Moderate: Soil with 0.1-0.2% SO4, water with 150-1500 ppm SO4
  - S2 — Severe: Soil with 0.2-2.0% SO4, water with 1500-10000 ppm SO4
  - S3 — Very Severe: Soil with >2.0% SO4, water with >10000 ppm SO4

Used in: ACI Table 6.3.4(b) - Maximum w/c ratio for sulfate exposure

HAS PRODUCTION DATA (≥30 tests)
-------------------------------
Description: Indicates if sufficient field test data is available for
standard deviation calculation.

  - Checked (default): Standard deviation σ = 3.5 MPa (assumed)
  - Unchecked: σ = 5.5 MPa (higher overdesign for limited data)

Used in: ACI Table 5.3.1.2 - Required average compressive strength

================================================================================
SECTION 7: IS OPTIONS (IS 10262 only)
================================================================================

EXPOSURE CLASS (IS 456)
------------------------
Description: Environmental exposure classification per IS 456:2000.

Options:
  - None: No exposure classification
  - Mild: Concrete surfaces protected against weather/aggressive exposure
  - Moderate: Concrete surfaces sheltered from severe rain/freezing
    Concrete in foundations, basement walls, etc.
  - Severe: Concrete surfaces exposed to severe rain, alternating wetting/drying
    Concrete in coastal areas, water tanks
  - Very Severe: Concrete surfaces exposed to sea water, aggressive soils/waters
    Concrete in immersion/tidal/splash zones
  - Extreme: Concrete surfaces in aggressive industrial environments
    Concrete in sea water, chemical plants

Used in: IS Table 4 - Minimum cement content, maximum w/c ratio

Note: Affects minimum cement content and maximum w/c ratio requirements.

================================================================================
SECTION 8: SUPPLEMENTARY CEMENTITIOUS MATERIAL (SCM)
================================================================================

SCM TYPE
--------
Description: Type of supplementary cementitious material to partially
replace Portland cement.

Options:
  - None: No SCM replacement [Default]
  - Fly Ash: Class F or Class C fly ash from coal combustion
  - GGBFS: Ground Granulated Blast Furnace Slag (from iron production)
  - Silica Fume: Ultra-fine particles from silicon/silicon alloy production

Benefits:
  - Improved durability
  - Reduced heat of hydration
  - Lower carbon footprint
  - Enhanced workability (fly ash)
  - Higher strength (silica fume)

REPLACEMENT (%)
---------------
Description: Percentage of cement replaced by SCM (by weight).

  - Fly Ash: Typically 15-35% (IS 456 allows up to 35%)
  - GGBFS: Typically 30-70%
  - Silica Fume: Typically 5-15%

Default: 0% (no replacement)

Note: SCM replacement affects:
  - Strength development rate
  - Heat of hydration
  - Durability properties
  - Specific gravity of cementitious blend

SPECIFIC GRAVITY
----------------
Description: Specific gravity of the SCM material.

Typical values:
  - Fly Ash: 2.00 - 2.50 (default: 2.20)
  - GGBFS: 2.80 - 3.00 (default: 2.90)
  - Silica Fume: 2.10 - 2.30 (default: 2.20)

Used in: Absolute volume calculations for blended cement

================================================================================
SECTION 9: ADMIXTURE
================================================================================

TYPE
----
Description: Chemical admixture to modify concrete properties.

Options:
  - None: No chemical admixture [Default]
  - Superplasticizer: High-range water reducer (HRWR)
    - Reduces water by 12-30%
    - Enables high-strength, self-consolidating concrete
  - Plasticizer: Mid-range water reducer (MRWR)
    - Reduces water by 8-15%
    - Improves workability without changing w/c
  - Retarder: Delays setting time
    - Useful for hot weather, long transport times
  - Accelerator: Speeds up setting/strength gain
    - Used in cold weather, emergency repairs
  - Air-Entraining: Creates stable air bubbles
    - Improves freeze-thaw resistance

DOSAGE (% by wt. cement)
------------------------
Description: Amount of admixture as percentage of cementitious material weight.

Typical ranges:
  - Superplasticizer: 0.5 - 2.0%
  - Plasticizer: 0.2 - 0.5%
  - Retarder: 0.1 - 0.4%
  - Accelerator: 1.0 - 4.0%

Default: 1.0%

WATER REDUCTION (%)
-------------------
Description: Percentage reduction in mixing water achievable with the
admixture while maintaining workability.

Typical values:
  - Superplasticizer: 15 - 30%
  - Plasticizer: 8 - 15%
  - Other types: 0%

Used in: Adjusting water content for target w/c ratio

Default: 0% (no water reduction)

================================================================================
NOTES
================================================================================

1. FIELD VISIBILITY
   - ACI Options group: Visible only when ACI 211.1 is selected
   - IS Options group: Visible only when IS 10262 is selected
   - Fineness Modulus: Visible only for ACI 211.1
   - Grading Zone: Visible only for IS 10262
   - Aggregate Shape: Visible only for IS 10262

2. DEFAULT VALUES
   Defaults are based on typical materials in Ghana/West Africa region.
   Adjust according to local material test results.

3. MATERIAL TESTING
   For accurate mix design, obtain:
   - Cement test certificate (type, specific gravity)
   - Aggregate sieve analysis (gradation)
   - Aggregate specific gravity test (SSD and OD)
   - Aggregate absorption test
   - Aggregate moisture content (field)
   - Bulk density test (coarse aggregate)

4. CALCULATION OUTPUT
   After clicking "Calculate Mix Design", the app generates:
   - Mix proportions per m³ (cement, water, aggregates)
   - W/C ratio determination
   - Absolute volume verification
   - Material quantities for specified volume
